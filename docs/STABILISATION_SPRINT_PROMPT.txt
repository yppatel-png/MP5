8. PHASE 5: Skill check, Practise and Progress must work as a loop
• Skill check: 8 to 12 adaptive questions chosen from the skills that matter for the student's goal, easier or harder depending on answers, early stop when confidence is enough. Show "Question 4 of about 10". The result screen shows a sorted bar chart of goal skills (strong, medium, gap) with a plain-language summary and the button "See my plan". Re-taking updates evidence; it never shows "1 of 3".
• Practise: the student always sees one skill, a short explanation (from the module text with the source page), then varied questions. After each answer show correct or not, the explanation, and the skill change ("Databases: 40% → 52%") plus what happens next. After a weak result, offer an easier set and a resource; after a strong result, move on.
• Progress: skills grouped by area with three states (not started, course done, proven by tests) shown by distinct shapes and labels; click a skill to see its evidence list (each attempt, score, source, weight). A "proven" skill needs enough tested items across more than one session; self-declared input alone can never mark a skill proven.
• Plan reacts: after practice or a simulated quiz (Admin or demo bar), the plan changes and a banner on Your plan explains the change with a diff.
