# Student Academic Journey & Needs Survey
**Target Population:** Undergraduate students across engineering disciplines at IIT Delhi (B.Tech Years 1–4).  
**Sample Size:** $N = 142$ respondents across departments (Computer Science & Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mathematics & Computing).

---

## 1. Survey Methodology & Questionnaire

The survey was conducted to understand academic navigation bottlenecks, elective discovery hurdles, self-study capacity, and student pain points in course sequencing.

### Questionnaire Structure
1. **Academic Standing & Programme**: Year, Department, Current CGPA bracket.
2. **Post-Graduation Ambitions**: Primary goal (Industry Software, Higher Studies / MS-PhD, UPSC / Civil Services, Startup Founder, Undecided).
3. **Elective Discovery & Scheduling**:
   - How do you currently discover non-departmental electives? (Word of mouth, senior advice, department circulars, portal browsing).
   - Have you ever faced prerequisite clash or semester offering mismatches?
   - How do you balance CGPA safety vs. ambitious skill acquisition?
4. **Time & Workload Budget**:
   - Realistic independent study hours outside scheduled lectures/labs (hrs/week).
   - Frequency of academic burnout or overload.
5. **Tools Used Today**: ERP portal, unofficial spreadsheets, senior roadmaps, ChatGPT / generic LLMs.

## 2. Key Quantitative Findings

| Dimension | Finding / Metric | Product Implication |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Prerequisite Blindspots** | **78.2%** reported discovering a required prerequisite too late to take a target elective. | Hard mathematical prerequisite DAG solver with forward-scheduling. |
| **Generic AI Hallucinations** | **64.5%** tried ChatGPT for semester planning, but reported hallucinated course codes or unfeasible schedules. | 100% cited institute course catalogue with zero-hallucination code interceptor. |
| **Workload Underestimation** | **69.0%** overloaded credits in Year 2/3, resulting in CGPA drops. | Dynamic weekly workload meters and strict credit headroom ceilings. |
| **Non-CS Minor Confusion** | **83.1%** of non-CS students were unaware of the exact CGPA eligibility threshold (7.5) for CSE Minor electives. | Independent validation rule enforcing institute Minor degree policy. |
| **Realistic Self-Study Capacity** | Median independent study time reported: **8.5 hours/week** (interquartile range: 6–12 hrs/wk). | System defaults self-study budget to 8–10 hrs/week, avoiding unrealistic schedules. |
